Flakey Fudge Brownies Recipe

Learn how to make Flakey Fudge Brownies to cover your craving!!

Ingredients

  • Maida - 105g
  • Cocoa Powder - 25g
  • Salt - 3g
  • Egg - 2 large, at Room Temperature
  • Brown Sugar - 90g
  • Caster Sugar - 80g
  • Oil - 120ml
  • Vanilla Extract - 1tsp
  • Dark Chocolate - 100g, melted

Instructions
  1. Mix all Dry Ingredients altogether
  2. Melt Dark Chocolate
  3. Beat Eggs and then Add Caster and Brown Sugar into this
  4. After that Add Oil, Vanilla Extract and Melted Chocolate
  5. Add Dry Mixture into this Batter and combine well
  6. Grease Parchment paper in tin and pour Batter into tin and tap the tin to remove bubbles
  7. Place tin in Pre heated Oven/ Pateela and bake for 20 minutes
  8. Take out and cool it

Serving
  • Brownies are ready to vanish your craving!!!. Enjoy them
